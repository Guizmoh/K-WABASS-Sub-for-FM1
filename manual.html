The K-WASUB manual

A modern bass synth for the M-VAVE FM-1: DnB, dub, dubstep, techno. Live wobble, two oscillators with two different engines, a sub, effects, an EQ, a morph between four sounds. Everything is played with the 27 keys, the four knobs K1–K4 and the FM-1's buttons.

K-WASUB is free. If it helps your sounds, a coffee keeps it going.Donate on Ko-fi

Key features

  • Two versions: A with 4 efficient engines for modern basses, B with 8 engines for deeper sound design (pads, synth and FX).
  • LFO learn: hold LFO and turn any knob, LFO 2 is assigned to that setting in one gesture (LFO 1 if you prefer, in the options).
  • CLASSIC or SHIFT (OCT− / OCT+) mode for a faster workflow, chosen in the options.
  • SEL windows: PERFORM, MORPH, FX LIVE, MACROS.
  • A/B morph: blend two sounds live.
  • A live LFO page to reshape your wobble basses while you play.
  • SEQ BASS, ARP, MIDI.
  • The sound: engines, filters, effects, master, and FX PADS.
  • Animated oscillator visuals, live.
  • A visual EQ.
  • Totally different themes, to fit the way you work.
  • Brightness, contrast, fonts and size.

And a lot more is coming. Don't hesitate to give your opinion and tell me which addition would be the most useful. A donation, even a small one, would help me enormously.

1 · Start in 3 minutes

  1. Install: open installer.html in Chrome or Edge, plug the FM-1 in with a USB data cable, click “Install K-WASUB”. Without a compatible browser: INSTALL-Linux.sh, INSTALL-Mac.command or INSTALL-Windows.bat (Python 3). To go back to KAWA or to the stock firmware: see the README.
  2. First start: K-WASUB asks you once how you want to play: CLASSIC (lists) or SHIFT (3 columns). After that, the start screen shows the FM-1 seen from the front; press any button.
  3. Play: the 27 keys play the bass. K1–K4 set the four settings shown. Turn PRESETS to change sound, on HOME or on the presets page (ARP): the change is immediate, with no confirmation (hold SAVE = UNDO if you regret it).
  4. Try: hold LFO and turn a knob to put LFO 2 on what that knob sets (or LFO 1: SYSTEM › LFO HELD = LFO). Hold FX: the keys become effects (FX PADS).

3 · The 8 pages and their views

EDIT · the oscillators

RESAMPLE, the DnB technique. Get a sound going (a held note with PLAY held, or the SEQ), go to the OSC C view, choose REC LENGTH (2, 3 or 4 s), then turn RESAMPLE towards +: the output (effects included) is recorded. When it ends, OSC C plays the recording (sample 16, RESAMPLE): looped or once (PLAY), slowed down (SPEED), transposed from the keys (PITCH › KEYS). Then change the base sound and go again: layer after layer. The recording is in RAM (11 kHz): it is lost at power-off.

5 · CLASSIC and SHIFT

Two ways to play (SYSTEM › UI MODE, or hold GLO). K-WASUB no longer asks at every start (SYSTEM › ASK brings the question back).

CLASSIC: lists. SELECT a row, ALGO its value, K1–K4 the first four rows. Simple, readable, you see the whole list.
SHIFT: 12 settings per page in three columns of four. Left: the knobs alone. Middle: OCT− held + knob. Right: OCT+ held + knob. The bottom of the screen reminds you which button does what. Short press on OCT−/OCT+: the octave. Long press without a knob: UNDO / REDO. Both together: PANIC.

6 · PERFORM: all the live playing on one screen

The first SEL window. Nothing to scroll: K1–K4 are the 4 macros, named after what they move (set in MACROS); SELECT and ALGO walk the dot of the MORPH X/Y; the wobble's speed and depth are on top. The keys play the bass; OCT+ held turns them into pads (the same as FX PADS) for as long as you hold it.

The SEL windows

SEL opens them one after the other, from the most played to the least played: PERFORM → MORPH X/Y → FX LIVE → MACROS → MORPH A/B → back. A page button closes the window.

MORPH, in practice. Continuous settings slide from one sound to the other; choices (engine, filter type, destination) switch to the nearest sound. A setting that is the same in every sound is left alone. MORPH X, Y and A/B are destinations: put LFO 1 on X and LFO 2 on Y and the sound circles between the four corners on the beat. SAVE keeps the result.

8 · Themes, palettes, brightness, contrast

The default look is STUDIO with the GOLD palette. SYSTEM › THEME changes the layout (CLASSIC, STUDIO, PURE, RACK, CLASSIC GLOW, TECH); PALETTE the colours (2 of each theme's own, plus SYNTHWAVE, MINT, BLACK / WHITE, INVERTED, RED, ICE, FLUO, PINK, GOLD, which work with every theme). CONTRAST (HIGH by default) makes the background truly black and the text truly white; MAX adds a darker gamma curve on the screen. FONT: 4 typefaces; LOUPE: the biggest size that fits.

TECH: the picture on almost the whole page

The TECH theme changes the layout completely: the oscillator, envelope, filter, LFO or effect fills almost the whole screen, under a thin bar; four tiles below are K1 to K4; one line shows the row chosen with SELECT / ALGO. The background is a gradient (top to bottom) and every window has its own, running another way: the picture left to right, the tiles bottom to top, the line right to left. Its own palettes: VOLT (fluo green) and CYBER (cyan, magenta).

CLASSIC GLOW: the simple layout, in gradients, fluo green

The CLASSIC GLOW theme (formerly GLOW) keeps the simplest layout and puts the colour in light: the accent glows down from the top of the screen, the chosen row lights up from the left, boxes and pictures are lit from above. Its own palettes are FLUO GREEN and TOXIC (yellow-green); every other palette works too.

Brightness

SYSTEM › BRIGHTNESS: ECO, NORMAL, BRIGHT and MAX. The FM-1's backlight is a plain switch (fully on, no steps): the low steps darken the colours, and the screen is also sent its brightness command (WRDISBV). MAX is the most the FM-1 can give: backlight full, screen at 255, pure white text, and every accent colour pushed to full intensity (same hue). It is the default.

9 · The sound

Engines, filters, modulation

8 engines per oscillator (version B; version A keeps ANALOG, REESE, WAVE and FM): ANALOG, SUPER / REESE, WAVETABLE (8 tables × 8 frames, you see the table), FM (operator positions), PHASE DIST, WAVEFOLDER, HARD SYNC, VOCAL. A and B add up, multiply (RING), modulate each other (X-MOD) or sync.
OSC C · SAMPLE: 10 very different sounds (808 BOOM, KICK, OI VOX, REESE LOOP, GROWL LOOP…) and 5 textures (VINYL, RAIN, WIND…), looped or once, slower or not without changing pitch.
Filter: LP 24, LP 12, BAND, HIGH, NOTCH, VOWEL, COMB, with drive. The mod lines make it an instrument: envelope, keyboard, velocity.
Presets: 51 factory sounds sorted by style (DNB, DUB, DUBSTEP, TECHNO, JUNGLE, GARAGE…), 32 slots of your own, favourites, UNDO / REDO over 16 steps.

The master: more bass, no volume surprises

What works on the low end and on the output:
MULTIBAND (OTT) (FX page, per sound): three bands (under 120 Hz, 120 Hz–2.5 kHz, above) each brought toward its own level: weak parts go up, loud parts go down. AMOUNT doses the effect, LOW BAND and HIGH BAND set the low and the high.
MONO BASS (MASTER, per sound, 120 Hz by default): under the chosen frequency, left and right get the same thing: a tight sub, even with chorus, delay or reverb.
SUB LOCK (SUB + NOISE, per sound, ON by default): the sub is oscillator A one or two octaves lower, in phase with it (its pitch, its drift, its fine): no cancelling, a solid low end.
DEEP BASS (OFF / DEEP / MAX): a shelf adding about +4 dB (MAX +7 dB) under 90 Hz, for more present and deeper bass.
Limiter (always on): nothing goes above the ceiling, even with the volume at full; the gain drops at once and comes back in ~50 ms.
AUTO LEVEL (ON by default): a slow normaliser that brings every sound toward the same average level (up in a few seconds, down in under a second, still in silence). The MASTER volume stays your final setting.

The effects

Saturation (4 types), compressor with PUMP, bitcrush, chorus, phaser, delay (4 types, locked to the tempo), reverb (4 types), glue, sub boost, 4-band EQ (LSHF, BELL, NOTCH, HSHF, high / low cut: K1–K4 the frequency, OCT− held the gain, OCT+ held the type, ALGO the width).

11 · Stability and troubleshooting

If the FM-1 switches off or restarts: at the next start, the cause shows in orange (LAST STOP, also in SYSTEM): WATCHDOG (TOO BUSY) processor overloaded · LOW VOLTAGE / 1.2V LOW weak power (battery, cable, USB port) · CRASH + address: a program error (write it down and send it).

Two cores: the voices run on core 1 and the effects on core 0. If core 1 cannot keep up, everything goes back to one core (AUDIO CORES in SYSTEM). A governor thins the sound out (reverb, voices) instead of crackling, and the effects' time is measured every block so the audio never blocks the rest. No sound? Check AUDIO CORES (try SINGLE), the MASTER volume and the SYSTEM page (CPU LOAD).

USB rescue: holding OCT− at power-on opens the rescue mode: the installer always works.

12 · Versions A and B

A: 4 efficient engines for modern basses (ANALOG, REESE, WAVE, FM), a simpler interface. B (install/version-b/): 8 engines (adds PHASE DIST, WAVEFOLDER, HARD SYNC, VOCAL), for deeper sound design (pads, synth and FX). Both have the same pages, themes and modes.
