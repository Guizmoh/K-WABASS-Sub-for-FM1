Turn your FM-1 into a modern bass synth

K-WASUB is a free firmware for the M-VAVE FM-1. It is made to build and play DnB, dub, dubstep and techno basses: growling reeses, deep subs, 808s, and above all a wobble you shape live with the knobs and the keys.

▶ Try it in the browser⬇ Install on the FM-1📖 Read the manual

What is it for?

Play a living bass

The 27 keys play the bass, K1–K4 set the four settings that matter on each page. Hold LFO and turn a knob: LFO 2 is wired to whatever that knob sets (or LFO 1, in the options).

Effects on the keys

FX PADS: white keys set the wobble speed (4, 8, 16, 24, 32, 48), black keys play stutters, tape stop, DJ filter, saturation, phaser…

A real bass sound

2 oscillators with 4 or 8 engines (reese, FM, wavetable…, your choice in the options), a sub locked on A, a sample layer and resample, 7 filters, multiband OTT, mono bass, compressor with pump, 4-band EQ, limiter and automatic level.

Morph between sounds

MORPH X/Y: four sounds in the corners of a square, a dot that blends them (an LFO can walk it). MORPH A/B: a fade on the beat.

Simple or fast

CLASSIC mode (lists) or SHIFT mode (3 columns, OCT− and OCT+ as Shift keys). 5 themes (CLASSIC GLOW and TECH with gradients), 13 palettes + their RAD gradient versions, 4 brightness levels.

Safe and open

Free, open source (GPL-3.0), can be reinstalled or removed over USB, with a rescue mode. If the FM-1 stops, the screen tells you why.

Try it in the browser

The same firmware, compiled for your browser: no FM-1 needed. Click the screen, then play with the computer keyboard, the mouse on the buttons, the wheel on the knobs. Sound starts at the first press.

Install on the FM-1

1Plug in

The FM-1 over USB to the computer, with a data cable (not a charge-only one). Close any other MIDI software.

2Open the installer

In Chrome or Edge: installer.html. Click “Install K-WASUB” and confirm. Unplug nothing until it is done.

3Play

After the restart, the start screen shows. Press any button. The manual covers the first 3 minutes.

⬇ Open the installerAll files (.fwsc)

Without Chrome or Edge: INSTALL-Windows.bat, INSTALL-Mac.command or INSTALL-Linux.sh (Python 3). You can go back to KAWA or to the stock firmware at any time; holding OCT− at power-on opens the USB rescue mode.

FAQ

Do I lose KAWA or the stock firmware?

No. Installation works like KAWA's, with the same loader: you can reinstall KAWA (kawa-0.5.fwsc) or the stock firmware (M-UPGRADE) whenever you like.

No sound on the FM-1?

In SYSTEM, look at AUDIO CORES (try SINGLE) and the MASTER volume. If the FM-1 switched off, read LAST STOP at start-up and note what it says.

4 or 8 engines?

There is one firmware. In SYSTEM › ENGINES you pick ESSENTIAL (4 efficient engines for modern basses: ANALOG, SUPER/REESE, WAVE, FM, the default) or ALL (8 engines for deeper sound design: pads, synth and FX sounds). A sound keeps its own engine either way.

What does “beta” mean?

The firmware is tested in the emulator and by automatic tests, but not yet on every FM-1. Tell me what you see (GitHub, Issues tab).
